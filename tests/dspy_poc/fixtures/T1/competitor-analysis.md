### 4. Switching Analysis
- **Lock-in factors:** Data portability issues; habit formation with current tracking methods
- **Switch triggers:** Privacy concerns, desire for community features, better motivation tools
- **Switching Cost:** Low

### 5. Competitive Gaps and Challenges
**Gaps (evidence-based):**
- **Validated - RUNSTR users:** "Would love to see anonymous leaderboards for different exercise types"
- **Validated - OwnLift users:** "Add community features like anonymous rankings"

**Challenges:**
- Market hardness: Fitness app market is saturated with established players
- User resistance: Many users are accustomed to existing tracking methods and may not switch without significant incentives
- Competitor advantage: Existing apps have network effects and brand recognition
- Development hurdle: Creating meaningful anonymous community features without compromising privacy
