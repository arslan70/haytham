## Market Intelligence

### **1. Market Context Summary**

- **Primary Category:** Online Therapy Platforms  
- **Adjacent Categories:** Mental Health Apps, Telemedicine Services  
- **Target Segment:** Psychologists managing private practices with existing patient bases

### **2. Jobs-to-be-Done Analysis**

**A. Core Jobs (customer perspective)**  
- Help me **facilitate structured wish-exchange sessions** for my existing patients using the “Power of 8” methodology  
- Help me **manage and track eight-person virtual sessions** (one receiver, seven givers) without administrative burden  
- Help me **maintain privacy and intimacy** in a closed, invite-only patient community  

**B. Job Dimensions**  
- **Functional:** Schedule, host, and record synchronous video/audio sessions for eight participants  
- **Emotional:** Foster a sense of connection and purpose for patients participating in wish-exchange  
- **Social:** Ensure the psychologist retains full control over session structure and participant selection  

**C. Current Solutions**  
- **Existing Tools:** Generic video conferencing tools (Zoom, Teams) lack session structuring for wish-exchange  
- **Frustrations:** Manual coordination of eight participants, lack of receiver/giver role assignment, no built-in methodology guidance  
- **Inadequacy:** Current tools don’t support the specific “Power of 8” format or closed-community patient access
