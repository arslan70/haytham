## Validate Before You Build

**Single Riskiest Assumption:** Psychologists will adopt the app if it structures wish-exchange sessions.  

**Low-Cost Experiments ($0-$500):**

1. **Experiment 1: Wish-Exchange Session Demand Survey**  
   - **Action:** Email 50 existing patients asking: “Would you participate in a 15-minute weekly wish-exchange session (one receiver, seven givers) focused on health/finance/happiness topics?”  
   - **Success Criteria:** >30% positive response  
   - **Failure Criteria:** <10% positive response  
   - **Cost:** $0 (email survey)  
   - **Timeline:** Week 1  

2. **Experiment 2: Psychologist Facilitation Willingness**  
   - **Action:** Interview 10 psychologists (including founder) about willingness to manage wish-exchange sessions using a simple tool (e.g., Google Forms + Zoom)  
   - **Success Criteria:** >50% express willingness  
   - **Failure Criteria:** <20% express willingness  
   - **Cost:** $200 (incentives for participants)  
   - **Timeline:** Week 2-3

## Pivot Options (if applicable)

**If Patient Demand is Low:**  
- **Pivot:** Shift to “Therapist-Led Group Coaching” — use the app for structured coaching sessions rather than wish-exchange  
- **What Changes:** Focus on professional development topics instead of health/finance/happiness  
- **What Stays:** Psychologist-controlled session orchestration, closed community  
- **Why:** Addresses the competitive gap in structured group sessions while leveraging existing therapist networks  

**If Psychologist Adoption is Low:**  
- **Pivot:** “Self-Guided Wish-Exchange” — patients run sessions themselves without psychologist facilitation  
- **What Changes:** Remove psychologist management layer, add AI facilitation tools  
- **What Stays:** Wish-exchange framework, closed community  
- **Why:** Reduces barrier to entry by eliminating psychologist time commitment  

**If HIPAA Costs are Prohibitive:**  
- **Pivot:** “Hybrid On-Premise + Cloud” — offer a downloadable app for local sessions + optional cloud for recording  
- **What Changes:** Session delivery model to reduce compliance burden  
- **What Stays:** Core wish-exchange structure, psychologist control  
- **Why:** Maintains value proposition while addressing regulatory risk
