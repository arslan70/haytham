# Validation Summary

## Executive Summary

The recommendation is PIVOT — critical gaps in revenue viability and market fit must be addressed before proceeding. The concept aims to develop an app for existing patients to gather in online wish-exchange sessions based on 'The Power of 8' methodology.

---

## Validation Findings

### Market Opportunity

The market for online therapy platforms is growing, with a significant demand for innovative approaches.

### Competition

Key competitors offer generic therapy sessions without structured wish-exchange formats.

### Critical Risks

- Revenue viability is uncertain
- Market fit for the unique wish-exchange format is unclear
