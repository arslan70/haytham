# Industry Benchmarks

Reference data for grounding financial projections. Select the archetype that matches `concept-anchor.json` and use these ranges as sanity checks. Flag projections that fall outside benchmark ranges.

## SaaS B2B

| Metric | Benchmark Range | Notes |
|--------|----------------|-------|
| Monthly churn | 1-3% | <1% is elite; >5% is a red flag |
| LTV/CAC ratio | 3:1 - 5:1 | <3:1 unsustainable for paid acquisition |
| Gross margin | 70-85% | Below 60% is unusual for SaaS |
| CAC payback | 12-18 months | >24 months signals capital-intensive growth |
| Net revenue retention | 100-130% | >120% indicates strong expansion revenue |
| ACV (SMB) | $1K-$10K/yr | Self-serve or low-touch sales |
| ACV (Mid-market) | $10K-$100K/yr | Inside sales motion |
| ACV (Enterprise) | $100K+/yr | Field sales, long cycles |

## SaaS B2C

| Metric | Benchmark Range | Notes |
|--------|----------------|-------|
| Monthly churn | 3-7% | Higher than B2B is normal |
| Viral coefficient | 0.3-0.7 | >1.0 enables organic-only growth |
| ARPU (monthly) | $5-$30 | Freemium models skew lower |
| Free-to-paid conversion | 2-5% | >10% is exceptional |
| Payback period | 1-6 months | Must be short due to high churn |

## Marketplace

| Metric | Benchmark Range | Notes |
|--------|----------------|-------|
| Take rate | 10-30% | Varies by vertical; services higher than goods |
| GMV growth (early) | 20-50% MoM | Post-liquidity; pre-liquidity is volatile |
| Supply/demand ratio | 3:1 - 10:1 supply:demand | Too low = poor selection; too high = supplier churn |
| Liquidity threshold | 30-60% match rate | Below 30% and users leave |
| Time to liquidity | 3-12 months | Per-city or per-vertical |

## E-Commerce / DTC

| Metric | Benchmark Range | Notes |
|--------|----------------|-------|
| Gross margin | 40-65% | Below 30% makes paid acquisition very hard |
| Repeat purchase rate | 20-40% within 90 days | First-time to repeat is the critical conversion |
| Average order value | Category dependent | Use as multiplier for revenue projections |
| Return rate | 10-30% | Apparel higher; digital near zero |
| CAC (paid social) | $15-$50 | Varies widely by category and channel |

## Consumer App

| Metric | Benchmark Range | Notes |
|--------|----------------|-------|
| DAU/MAU ratio | 20-50% | >50% is strong daily habit; <15% is weak retention |
| D1 retention | 25-40% | Below 20% signals poor onboarding or weak hook |
| D7 retention | 10-20% | The critical inflection point |
| D30 retention | 5-10% | Surviving users form the core base |
| ARPU (ad-supported) | $0.50-$3/month | Highly geography-dependent |
| ARPU (subscription) | $5-$15/month | Consumer willingness ceiling is real |

## Developer Tool

| Metric | Benchmark Range | Notes |
|--------|----------------|-------|
| GitHub stars growth | 100-500/month (early traction) | Vanity metric but useful directional signal |
| Free-to-paid conversion | 1-5% | OSS-to-paid is typically lower than SaaS |
| Enterprise adoption | 5-15% of paid base | Long sales cycle; drives majority of revenue |
| Time to first value | <5 minutes | Critical for developer adoption; >15 min is a barrier |
| Community-to-revenue lag | 6-18 months | Stars/users precede revenue significantly |

## Open Source / Community

| Metric | Benchmark Range | Notes |
|--------|----------------|-------|
| GitHub stars growth | 50-300/month (early traction) | Compound signal with downloads for real adoption |
| Contributor retention (6mo) | 5-15% of first-time contributors | Most contributors are one-time; 15%+ retention is strong |
| Time to first contribution | <30 minutes setup | Higher friction = lower contributor pipeline |
| npm/PyPI weekly downloads growth | 20-50% MoM (early) | Package manager downloads are stronger signal than stars |
| Issue response time | <48 hours | Slower response signals abandoned/understaffed project |
| Fork-to-star ratio | 0.1-0.3 | Higher ratio suggests utility; lower suggests novelty |
| Community-to-revenue lag | 12-24 months | Longer than developer tools due to OSS expectations |

## Internal Tool / Productivity

| Metric | Benchmark Range | Notes |
|--------|----------------|-------|
| Time saved per user | 2-10 hrs/week | Must be measurable and believable |
| Adoption rate (org) | 30-60% within 6 months | Below 20% signals poor problem-market fit |
| ROI threshold | 3-5x cost | Buyers typically need clear ROI justification |
| Deployment time | Days to weeks | Months-long deploys reduce willingness to buy |
| Seat expansion rate | 20-40%/year | Land-and-expand is the typical growth motion |
