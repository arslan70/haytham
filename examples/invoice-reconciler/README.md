# Automated Invoice-to-PO Matching for SMBs

**Idea:** A B2B SaaS that automatically matches incoming invoices to purchase orders for small and medium businesses, flagging discrepancies and reducing manual reconciliation.

**Verdict:** PIVOT

**Composite Score:** 2.4 / 5.0

This example matters because Haytham said no. The idea sounds reasonable, the market is real, and the pain exists. But the validation found that the AP automation space is mature and well-defended. QuickBooks and Xero already handle basic matching for SMBs. Enterprise players like Tipalti, BILL.com, and Coupa own the rest. A generic invoice-matching SaaS for SMBs would be fighting for scraps in a market where incumbents have distribution advantages that a startup can't overcome on product quality alone.

Haytham recommended pivoting to a **vertical-specific** approach: invoice-to-PO matching for construction subcontractors, where progress billing, lien waivers, and change-order tracking create workflow complexity that generic tools handle poorly. The founder agreed, and Phases 2-4 reflect the pivoted idea.

This is a good example of the system doing its job. Validating everything would be worse than validating nothing.
