# Gym Community Leaderboard with Anonymous Handles

**Idea:** A mobile-first app where gym-goers compete on leaderboards using anonymous handles. Users log workouts manually, follow other handles, and compare performance across gym-specific metrics.

**Verdict:** GO with HIGH risk

**Composite Score:** 3.2 / 5.0

The opportunity is real (gym-goers already track and compare informally), but two risks dominate: the cold-start problem (leaderboards are useless without users) and anonymity limiting viral growth (you can't share your rank if nobody knows who you are). The recommendation is to validate the cold-start problem before building.
